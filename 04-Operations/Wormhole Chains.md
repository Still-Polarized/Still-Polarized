# Wormhole Chains

## The loop

1. **Hunt** — find as many wormholes as we can
2. **Chain** — connect them into a small chain
3. **Run** — find ratting sites and run them (see [[Ratting & Roaching]])
4. **Fight** — look for small corps to fight inside the chain (see [[PvP]])
