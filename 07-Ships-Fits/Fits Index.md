# Fits Index

Standard corp fits, ready to copy. Each fit has a **Copy** button at the top‑right of its block — click it, then paste straight into the [EVE Fitting Tool](https://www.eve-fitting-tool.com/). Which fit belongs to which doctrine: see [[Ship Doctrines]].

> **How to import:** click **Copy**, open EFT (or your fitting tool), and **paste**. The first line of each block is the fit name, e.g. `[Ikitursa, *Corp Brawler]`.
