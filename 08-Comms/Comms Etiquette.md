# Comms Etiquette

- **TS is for ops** — keep idle chatter to Discord
- **Pings:** TBC (who may ping whom — e.g. leaders / officers only for @everyone)

- **Kill posts:** short is fine — link the entry in [[Kills]]
- **General:** don't be an arsehole, don't spam (see [[Code of Conduct]])
