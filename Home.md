# Still Polarized

> **Wormhole Corp · Low Sec** · Tag: `[STLPO]` · Language: English (UK)

**Still Polarized** is a lowsec-focused exploration and ops corp. We are based in **Bayuka** (Derelik) and live in wormhole space: hunting wormholes, running small chains, ratting and roaching, huffing and mining in our home lowsec, and looking for small corps to fight.
