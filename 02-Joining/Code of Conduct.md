# Code of Conduct

The entire code of conduct fits in one line:

> **Don't be an arsehole. No scamming.**

## What that means in practice

- No scamming — ever. No corp wallet skimming, no PLEX scams, no "trade my fit, keep my ship"
- No highsec griefing or attention-seeking
- No toxicity in comms — keep it to comms, keep it civil
- Don't sell or transfer corp assets (ships, fits, stations, modules) without leader approval
- If you're unsure whether something is cool, ask a leader before doing it

## Discipline

- **Talk to a leader.** There is no discipline ladder — any member with a problem (or who has caused one) talks to a leader and we sort it out.
- Two leaders cover UK + US, so issues get a response either way
