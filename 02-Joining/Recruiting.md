# Recruiting

## Who we want

- Similar interests: wormhole exploration, lowsec ops, running small chains
- **Good attitude** — this matters more than anything. We will take anyone with similar interests and a good attitude.

## Requirements

- **No hard skill or TQ minimum.**
- **Must be able to do at least one of:** huffing, mining, ratting, roaching, drone fabs, PvP
  - Typical ships per activity: huffing — Prospect · mining — Covetor · ratting/roaching — Marauder and T3 cruisers · fabs — Marauder / Dreadnought · PvP — must be able to fit a Cov-ops
- 30-day trial period before full membership

## The process

1. **Interview** — tell us who you are, your timezone, what you can run (per the list above), and what you're looking for in a corp
2. **30-day trial** — fly with us, sit in comms, see how we operate
3. **Full membership** — you're in

## How to apply

We recruit via open recruitment posts:

- **EVE Forums:** <https://forums.eveonline.com/t/still-polarized-the-worst-wormhole-corp-youll-ever-hear-of/520422>
- **Reddit (r/evejobs):** <https://www.reddit.com/r/evejobs/comments/1x8mijs/still_polarized_the_worst_wormholers_you_will/>

Apply through either post, then follow the process above.
